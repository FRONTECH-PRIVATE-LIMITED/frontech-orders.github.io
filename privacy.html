Who we are

FT Orders is the internal sales-order app of Frontech Private Limited, used by Frontech staff to place and process distributor orders. It is not offered to the public.

Daily stock report (Gmail)

"Frontech Stock Import" connects, with read-only permission, to one company mailbox: frontech.report@gmail.com. Once an hour each morning it looks for the "Daily Report" mail sent by Frontech's own report server, reads the attached stock spreadsheet (dailyreporter.xlsx) and updates the stock shown in FT Orders.

  • It does not send, delete, move or change any email.
  • Only the stock figures from that attachment are stored. Other emails are not read or stored.
  • Data obtained through Google APIs is used only for this purpose, is not shared with or sold to anyone, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models. Our use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's security settings (Third-party connections).

Staff data in FT Orders

FT Orders stores what is needed to run orders: staff name, login code, branch and role, the orders, messages and invoice files entered in the app. Data is kept on Frontech-managed services (Google Apps Script & Sheets, and Supabase in the Mumbai region) and is accessible only to Frontech staff according to their role.
